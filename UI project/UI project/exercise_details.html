EXERCISE GUIDE

Learn how to perform each exercise correctly and safely.

Squats

Exercise Information

Muscle: Legs

Equipment: Bodyweight

Difficulty: Beginner

How to Perform

  1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Lower your hips slowly.
  3. Keep your back straight.
  4. Stand back up.

Safety Tips

Keep your back straight and control the movement.

Recommended Workout

Sets: 3

Reps: 12

Chest Press

Exercise Information

Muscle: Chest

Equipment: Dumbbells

Difficulty: Beginner

How to Perform

  1. Lie on the bench.
  2. Hold the weights near your chest.
  3. Push the weights up.
  4. Lower them slowly.

Safety Tips

Use a comfortable weight and control the movement.

Recommended Workout

Sets: 3

Reps: 12

T-Bar Row

Exercise Information

Muscle: Back

Equipment: T-Bar Machine

Difficulty: Intermediate

How to Perform

  1. Stand in a stable position.
  2. Hold the handles.
  3. Pull the weight toward your body.
  4. Lower it slowly.

Safety Tips

Keep your back straight and avoid swinging the weight.

Recommended Workout

Sets: 3

Reps: 12

Equipment: Cable Machine

Difficulty: Beginner

Biceps Curl

Exercise Information

Muscle: Biceps

Equipment: Dumbbells

Difficulty: Beginner

How to Perform

  1. Stand straight with dumbbells.
  2. Keep your elbows close to your body.
  3. Curl the weights upward.
  4. Lower them slowly.

Safety Tips

Avoid swinging your body during the exercise.

Front Shoulder Raise

Exercise Information

Muscle: Shoulders

Equipment: Dumbbells

Difficulty: Beginner

How to Perform

  1. Stand straight with dumbbells.
  2. Keep your elbows slightly bent.
  3. Raise your arms forward.
  4. Lower them slowly.

Safety Tips

Avoid swinging the weights and maintain good posture.
