EXERCISE GUIDE

Squats

Learn how to perform this exercise.

Exercise Information

Muscle: Legs

Equipment: Bodyweight

Difficulty: Beginner

How to Perform

  1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Lower your hips slowly.
  3. Keep your back straight.
  4. Stand back up.

Safety Tips

Keep your back straight and control the movement.

Recommended Workout

Sets: 3

Reps: 12
